05 · Join us

Bring mathematics to a question that matters.

I am actively recruiting undergraduate students, graduate students, and postdocs to join my research group. Interested candidates are encouraged to email yipinglu [at] bicmr.pku.edu.cn.

Open pathways

Ways to work with the group.

Strong candidates may come from mathematics, statistics, computer science, operations research, engineering, or related fields.

01

Undergraduate researchers

Projects can begin with a focused reading plan and a concrete computational or theoretical question. Preparation in probability, analysis, optimization, or numerical methods is helpful.

02

Graduate students

We welcome students interested in machine learning theory, applied probability, computational mathematics, scientific machine learning, and generative inference.

03

Postdoctoral researchers

Postdoctoral projects should connect a clear mathematical contribution to an important computational or scientific problem.

Contact

Identify one direction or paper from this site that genuinely interests you and explain the question you would like to explore.
